# Miriam College HRIS — Game-Changer Features (Proposal)

**Purpose:** features beyond the baseline TOR modules that would make the panel (PMO, HR Director, ICT Director, Comp & HR Officer — i.e. *management*) see Miriam-fit, not a generic HRIS. Each is mapped to the TOR and demo-able in the front-end prototype.

Legend — **🟢 Build now (Tier 1)** · 🟡 Roadmap (Tier 2) · ⚪ Future

---

## 🟢 1. Management / Executive Command Center *(new "Management" login)*
A strategic, read-only portal for VPs, directors, and the President's office.
- AI **executive summary** of the workforce in plain language
- Strategic KPIs: total workforce, YoY attrition, **annual workforce cost**, engagement index, time-to-fill, faculty : staff ratio
- **Predictive analytics**: attrition forecast, **succession readiness**, retirement wave, manpower projection
- **Campus comparison** (QC vs Nuvali vs Alviera) side-by-side
- Diversity & inclusion snapshot
- Risk / watchlist with recommended actions
- **Why game-changer:** the people deciding on this bid *are* management. This is the screen that sells the system to them.
- **TOR:** "Executive dashboards with workforce trends and strategic HR insights"; "Predictive analytics on turnover, retirement, succession, and manpower"; AI with human oversight.

## 🟢 2. Interactive Org Chart & Workforce Map
A living, clickable organizational chart across all three campuses.
- Drill-down hierarchy: leadership → divisions → departments → people
- **Vacancy highlighting** (open requisitions shown on the chart), span-of-control
- Campus filter; click a node for department detail
- **Why game-changer:** instantly shows the multi-campus structure and reporting lines — something spreadsheets can't. Very visual in a presentation.
- **TOR:** multi-campus operations; "reporting structure"; "employee movement"; automatic profile updates on reporting changes.

## 🟢 3. Digital Onboarding Journey *(closes the Applicant → Employee loop)*
When an applicant **accepts an offer**, a guided onboarding kicks in.
- Onboarding checklist: contract e-signing, **auto-created 201 File**, IT & email provisioning, **biometric enrollment**, orientation, benefits auto-enrollment
- Progress tracking; **auto-provisioning** of attendance account + payroll record
- **Why game-changer:** this is the literal proof of the TOR's #1 promise — *no manual re-encoding* between Talent Acquisition, Onboarding, 201, Timekeeping, and Payroll.
- **TOR:** "Seamless transfer of employee information from Talent Acquisition and Onboarding... without manual encoding"; "Integration of onboarding into creation of attendance accounts and payroll records."

## 🟢 4. Workforce Cost & Budget Simulator (What-If Planning)
An interactive scenario planner for Finance & management.
- Sliders: **merit increase %**, new hires, promotions, allowance adjustments
- Live recompute of **projected annual personnel cost**, Budget vs Actual, variance
- Breakdown by campus / employment type; save scenarios
- **Why game-changer:** turns payroll data into forward-looking decisions — a board-level tool.
- **TOR:** "Personnel budget preparation and forecasting"; "Budget vs Actual"; "Forecasting of payroll expenses and workforce costs"; "cost-optimization recommendations."

## 🟢 5. Biometric Time-In Kiosk (M10 / Facial simulator)
A full-screen kiosk mode (launchable from the login screen).
- Tap-to-time-in with **M10 Fingerprint / Facial / RFID** tabs
- Simulated scan → shows employee, greeting, and time-in; syncs to Timekeeping
- **Why game-changer:** makes the biometric integration *tangible* on screen instead of a bullet point.
- **TOR:** "Employee time-in/out through biometric devices (M10 Fingerprint and Facial Biometrics)… or other supported devices."

## 🟢 6. Philippine Compliance & Government Reporting Center
A single hub for statutory, education, and labor compliance — PH-specific, not generic.
- **BIR** (2316, 1601-C, Alphalist), **SSS** (R-3), **PhilHealth** (RF-1), **Pag-IBIG** (MCRF), **DOLE**, **CHED** faculty reports
- Compliance calendar with due dates; one-click generation; **Data Privacy Act** status
- **Why game-changer:** shows the bidder understands the *Philippine* regulatory reality of a college.
- **TOR:** "BIR reportorial requirements"; "statutory reports"; "Compliance with applicable Philippine data privacy and other relevant requirements."
