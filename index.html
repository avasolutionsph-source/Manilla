About Miriam College

A premier Catholic institution forming leaders of competence and conscience.

Founded by the Maryknoll Sisters of New York in 1926, Miriam College offers academic programs at the basic and tertiary levels through its main campus in Loyola Heights, Quezon City, with growing campuses in MC Nuvali (Calamba, Laguna) and MC Alviera (Porac, Pampanga).

Across all campuses, the institution upholds excellent educational standards through continuous curriculum enhancement, strategic international and local partnerships, a competent faculty base, and strengthened teaching in Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics (STEAM) — preparing students to thrive in the fields of the future.

What we stand for

Our Core Values

The four pillars that guide every member of the Miriam community.

Veritas

The power of knowledge and the liberating force of truth — pursued with fairness, openness, and the highest academic integrity.

Justice

A society where power and opportunity are shared, rights are upheld, and gender equality and human dignity are centrally valued.

Peace

Peace-builders committed to non-violence, creative conflict resolution, and caring relationships among all people and with creation.

Integrity of Creation

Stewards of all creation, caring for the earth and practicing a sustainable lifestyle that sustains the health of the planet.
