01 — About

A century of forming leaders of competence and conscience.

Veritas · 1926
100
Years toward the Miriam Centennial

Founded by the Maryknoll Sisters of New York in 1926, Miriam College offers academic programs at the basic and tertiary levels through its main campus in Loyola Heights, Quezon City — with growing campuses in MC Nuvali (Calamba, Laguna) and MC Alviera (Porac, Pampanga).

Across all campuses, the institution upholds excellent educational standards through continuous curriculum enhancement, strategic international and local partnerships, a competent faculty base, and strengthened teaching in Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics (STEAM).

Our Mission

We shape the whole person through Christ-centered education, empowering independent and lifelong learners committed to serving the greater good.

Vision — A thriving future-making learning community that forms discerning individuals who live with purpose and shape a better world.

02 — Values

The four pillars that guide every member of our community.

i.

Veritas

The power of knowledge and the liberating force of truth — pursued with fairness, openness, and the highest academic integrity.

ii.

Justice

A society where power and opportunity are shared, human dignity upheld, and gender equality centrally valued.

iii.

Peace

Peace-builders committed to non-violence, creative conflict resolution, and caring relationships with all creation.

iv.

Integrity of Creation

Stewards of all creation, caring for the earth and sustaining the health of the planet for generations to come.
