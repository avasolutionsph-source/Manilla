## Introduction

Miriam College seeks to procure and implement an integrated **Human Resources Information System (HRIS)** that will support the efficient, secure, and streamlined management of its human resources operations and employee lifecycle. The HRIS is intended to:

- Establish a **centralized and reliable source** of employee information
- Automate HR workflows and approvals
- Minimize manual and duplicate data entry
- Improve the accuracy and timeliness of HR transactions
- Strengthen accountability and auditability
- Provide **self-service tools, reports, dashboards, and analytics** to employees, supervisors, HR personnel, and management

The upgraded HRIS is expected to support operations across the Quezon City, Nuvali, and Alviera campuses.

## Objective

Establish an integrated, secure, and efficient HRIS that serves as the school's centralized platform for managing and delivering HR services throughout the employee lifecycle. It shall enable automation and integration of HR processes, improve accuracy/accessibility/timeliness, strengthen internal controls, and provide reliable data, reporting, analytics, and decision-support — plus a **scalable and sustainable** platform that adapts to evolving requirements.

The solution shall also support appropriate **Artificial Intelligence (AI)-enabled** capabilities, subject to the College's data privacy, information security, governance, and human oversight requirements.

## 1. System Specification — Modules

### A. Recruitment & Applications

**1. Recruitment Module**
- Personnel requisition creation, approval workflow, and tracking
- Job vacancy posting through internal and external recruitment channels
- Applicant Tracking System (ATS) with centralized candidate database
- Resume collection, screening, and candidate profiling
- Candidate shortlisting, interview scheduling, evaluation, and assessment management
- Recruitment pipeline monitoring and status tracking
- Job offer generation and approval workflow
- Seamless integration with onboarding and employee records management
- Recruitment analytics and reporting (time-to-fill, source of hire, applicant conversion rates, recruitment effectiveness)
- Automated notifications and communication with applicants, hiring personnel, recruiters
- Mobile-accessible, user-friendly interface

**2. AI Requirements for Talent Acquisition** (built-in or via integration)
- Automated resume screening and parsing
- Candidate matching and ranking based on job requirements and competencies
- Intelligent candidate recommendations from talent pools
- Recruitment analytics and predictive hiring insights
- AI-assisted job description generation
- Recruitment chatbot / virtual assistant for candidate inquiries
- AI-powered reporting and dashboard insights

### B. Employee Records Management (Employee 201 File)

> **Particular:** Seamless transfer of employee information from Talent Acquisition & Onboarding into Employee Records (201 File) and Timekeeping — **without manual encoding**.

- Integration of onboarding into creation of employee profiles
- Personal Information · Employment Information · Service Record
- Family Background · Educational Background · Government/Licensure Exam
- Work History · Achievement/Award · Professional Organization · Training/Seminars
- Medical History with upload feature for annual physical exam results
- Audit trail of transactions
- Trigger for end-of-contract dates & others
- Other relevant information for employee profile

### C. Schedule Management / Timekeeping & Attendance

- Integration of onboarding into creation of attendance accounts upon successful onboarding
- Synchronization of employment details, org assignment, position, department, work location, employment status, and work schedule from employee master database
- Time entry / work schedule creation & management: regular, flexible, shifting, hybrid, compressed
- Seamless integration with Payroll for automated inputs & computation
- Time-in/time-out via **biometric devices (M10 Fingerprint & Facial Biometrics)**, web portals, mobile apps, GPS-enabled attendance, or other devices
- Real-time attendance monitoring & workforce visibility dashboards

**1. Application Filing** — Leave, Official Business, Overtime, Work Hours Offsetting, and others
- Automated computation of worked hours, tardiness, undertime, overtime, holiday pay, night differential, rest day work, leaves, attendance correction, authorization, approval workflows based on configurable policies
- Employee & supervisor self-service access to attendance records, schedules, transactions
- Configurable attendance rules, labor standards, company-specific policies
- Standard & customizable attendance/workforce/compliance reports
- Mobile-accessible interface; auto-update of attendance profiles on status/department/designation/location/reporting changes
- Complete audit trail of attendance-related actions & movement history

**2. AI Requirements for 201 File & Timekeeping**
- Automated attendance anomaly detection & alerts
- Identification of habitual tardiness, absenteeism, attendance patterns
- Predictive absenteeism & workforce attendance trend analysis
- Predictive overtime utilization & staffing requirements
- AI-powered analytics & dashboards for workforce productivity

### D. Payroll & Compensation Management

**1. Payroll Module**
- Salary & compensation structure (revisable/alterable: salary adjustment, performance bonus, etc.)
- Tax deductions incl. BIR reportorial requirements; loan deductions (SSS, Pag-IBIG, MCC, additional benefit acquisitions)
- Benefits administration & management
- Integration with approved leave transactions affecting payroll
- Automatic creation/maintenance of payroll records upon onboarding
- Management of allowances, incentives, bonuses, deductions, loans, other earnings
- Multiple payroll groups & schedules
- Payroll simulation, validation, reconciliation, audit trails
- Automated payslips & statutory reports
- Employee self-service access to payroll & compensation records
- Payroll special processing · Issuance of **2316** · **Journal Entries (for Accounting SAP system)**

**2. Performance Management & Faculty Ranking Integration**
- Performance-based salary adjustments & merit increases
- Salary adjustments from approved promotions & reclassifications
- Integration with Faculty Ranking System for rank-based salary increases
- Configurable salary-increase triggers: performance ratings, faculty rank promotions, position promotions, salary grade movements, management-approved adjustments

**3. Workforce Cost Planning & Budget Management**
- Personnel budget preparation & forecasting
- Projection/staff-cost planning of salaries, benefits, allowances, incentives, mandatory contributions (by unit, cost center, employment type)
- Budget utilization monitoring & staff-cost tracking (Budget vs Actual)
- Real-time workforce cost dashboards & reporting

**4. Workflow Integration & Notifications**

**5. AI Requirements for Payroll & Compensation**
- Payroll anomaly & error detection
- Automated payroll validation & compliance monitoring
- Salary benchmarking & compensation analysis
- Pay equity & compensation trend analysis
- Forecasting of payroll expenses & workforce costs
- Budget utilization & overrun prediction
- Variance analysis & cost-optimization recommendations
- Payroll exceptions & discrepancies

### E. Benefits Administration Module

> **Particular:** Native AI capabilities or seamless AI integration to improve efficiency, employee experience, and decision-making.

**1. Benefits Module**
- Centralized management of benefits & eligibility
- Automatic enrollment upon hiring & onboarding
- Management/monitoring of mandatory & company-sponsored benefits
- Administration of health, medical, insurance, retirement, wellness, educational, & other programs
- Eligibility tracking by status/tenure/rank/position & configurable criteria
- Automated computation of employee & employer contributions
- Tracking of utilization & balances
- Beneficiary & dependent management
- Electronic submission/approval/monitoring of benefit claims
- Configurable workflows for applications, approvals, enrollment, updates
- Payroll integration for automatic deductions & contributions
- Employee self-service; history maintained in digital 201 File
- Automated notifications (enrollment, renewals, expirations, claim status, eligibility)
- Secure electronic storage/retrieval of benefit records

**2. Benefits Monitoring & Administration** — dashboards, real-time availment/utilization, cost monitoring, dependents eligibility, renewals, expiry monitoring, statements & summaries

**3. Reporting & Analytics** — benefits register, enrollment, utilization, claims, contribution, cost analysis, eligibility, dependents, dept/org dashboards, historical trends

**4. Workflow & Notifications** — onboarding, regularization, promotion/status changes, enrollment/renewal, eligibility changes, claims processing, coverage/cert expiry, separation & offboarding

### F. Performance Management System (PMS)

> **Particular:** Integrate the in-house developed PMS framework.

**1. PMS** — goal setting & KPI alignment, online evaluation & approval workflow, Individual Development Plans, performance dashboards & analytics, performance improvement monitoring
**2. Faculty Ranking & Promotion System** — automated ranking/promotion computation, tracking of academic qualifications/research/training/teaching performance/institutional contributions, promotion eligibility monitoring, faculty portfolio & ranking history
**3. Learning & Development Integration** — TNA from performance & competency gaps, training nomination/approval/monitoring, learning records & certification tracking, training effectiveness & ROI, recommended learning pathways
**4. Payroll & Compensation Integration** — salary adjustments/promotions/merit tracking, benchmarking analytics, internal equity & compression analysis, workforce cost & compensation forecasting
**5. Employee Records Management** — centralized digital 201 files, movement/service records, contract/license/cert monitoring, retirement & tenure tracking, self-service portal
**6. AI Capability** — AI HR Assistant, competency gap identification, personalized training recommendations, promotion/career readiness insights, predictive analytics (turnover, retirement, succession, manpower), executive dashboards

### G. Offboarding, Clearance & Separation Management

**1. Employee Self-Service Offboarding Portal** — online resignation/separation filing, automated clearance initiation, real-time clearance tracking, digital submission of requirements, exit interview management, final pay processing monitoring, automated separation documents
**2. Integrated Clearance Workflow** — electronic routing to: immediate supervisors, HR, Payroll, Finance & Accounting, IT Office, Property & Supply, Library, Security Office, Academic Units, Research Offices, and other designated offices
**3. Automated Triggers** (resignation/retirement/end-of-contract/termination/separation) — clearance workflow, asset accountability verification, benefits & payroll validation, exit interview scheduling, records archiving, final pay processing
**4. AI Capability** — turnover trend analysis, attrition risk monitoring, exit interview analysis, retention insights, workforce planning forecasts

## 2. Cross-Cutting Requirements

### User Interface
Consistent, intuitive, accessible experience for HR administrators, employees, supervisors, applicants, and other authorized users. Must support multi-campus operations.

### Process Automation, Workflow & Notifications
- Automated review & approval processes · Employee self-service transactions
- Automated email / in-system / mobile notifications · Workflow audit trails
- Customizable workflows per HR Division · **HRIS mobile application**

### Analytics, Reporting & Data Extraction
- Dashboards & reports per module · Automated/scheduled extractions in approved formats
- HR metrics & reporting tools · Standard + configurable/custom reports
- Filter, drill-down, export (subject to access permission)

### Customization & Configurability
- Configure functions without extensive custom programming
- Add/modify/remove users & access roles · Adapt processes to future changes
- Configure forms, fields, approval routes, notifications, reports, business rules

### Storage, Security, Privacy & Recovery
- Secure access to confidential/sensitive data · **MFA** · Role-based authorization
- Encryption in transit & at rest · Backup & recovery · Audit logging
- Secure handling of uploaded documents (incl. medical) · **PH Data Privacy Act** compliance
- Disclosure of hosting architecture, data location, third-party processors
- Defined security incident & breach notification procedures

### Deployment, Hosting & Technical Architecture
- Cloud-based option · Mobile (iOS/Android) · API integration for institutional systems
- Cloud hosting details (architecture, location, availability, backup, DR, recurring cost)
- On-premises option (server/network/DB/OS/storage/security/backup) · Scalability

### Integration Requirements *(core requirement)*
- Integration among Recruitment, Onboarding, Employee Records, Timekeeping, Payroll, Benefits, PMS, Faculty Rank & Evaluation, L&D, Offboarding
- **Integration with Accounting SAP system** for approved journal entries & financial interfaces
- Integration with biometric / attendance devices · APIs or secure integration
- Documented data mapping, interface specs, validation, error handling, reconciliation, monitoring
- Secure authentication for system-to-system integration

### Data Migration & Implementation
Project initiation & plan · Requirements validation & fit-gap · Configuration & approved customization · Data-migration (planning, mapping, cleansing, test migration, validation, reconciliation, production) · Integration dev & E2E testing · Functional/security/integration/UAT · Go-live/cutover & rollback · Post-go-live hypercare · Documentation & knowledge transfer

### Training & Support
- Comprehensive training for admins & all user groups (HR, payroll, benefits, recruitment, performance, faculty ranking, L&D, ICT, finance/accounting, supervisors, employees)
- User/admin/technical guides & training materials · Support during dev & after go-live
- Channels: phone, chat, email, onsite, remote · Minimum **8×5**, 24/7 where available
- Defined response/resolution targets by severity · **1-year & 3-year support options** · Release/patch/upgrade notifications

### AI Governance & Responsible Use
- The College retains **human oversight** over decisions affecting employment, compensation, performance, ranking, promotion, benefits eligibility, separation
- Identify each AI capability: native / integrated / third-party / proposed-roadmap
- Identify AI model/provider & hosting/processing arrangements
- Disclose whether school data/prompts/documents/outputs are retained or used to train models
- Access controls & safeguards against unauthorized disclosure
- **AI recommendations shall not auto-execute** employment/compensation/ranking/promotion/disciplinary/separation decisions without authorized human approval
- Describe limitations, validation practices, auditability, human review mechanisms
