## Institutional snapshot
- **Founded:** 1926, by the **Maryknoll Sisters of New York**.
- **Campuses:** Quezon City (main, Loyola Heights) · MC Nuvali (Calamba, Laguna) · MC Alviera (Porac, Pampanga).
- **Mission:** "We shape the whole person through Christ-centered education, empowering independent and lifelong learners committed to serving the greater good."
- **Vision:** "A thriving future-making learning community that forms discerning individuals who live with purpose and shape a better world."
- **Core Values:** **Veritas** · **Justice** · **Peace** · **Integrity of Creation**
- **Contact:** Katipunan Avenue, Loyola Heights, Quezon City 1108 · (02) 8930-6272 · assist@mc.edu.ph · www.mc.edu.ph
