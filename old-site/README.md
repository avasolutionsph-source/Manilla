## Institutional snapshot (quick reference)
- **Founded:** 1926, by the **Maryknoll Sisters of New York**. Premier Catholic educational institution.
- **Campuses:** Quezon City (main, Loyola Heights), **Nuvali** (Calamba, Laguna), **Alviera** (Porac, Pampanga).
- **Mission:** "We shape the whole person through Christ-centered education, empowering independent and lifelong learners committed to serving the greater good."
- **Vision:** "A thriving future-making learning community that forms discerning individuals who live with purpose and shape a better world."
- **Core Values:** **Veritas** (Truth) · **Justice** · **Peace** · **Integrity of Creation**
- **Units:** Basic Education Unit (Preschool, Grade School, JHS, SHS) and Higher Education Unit.
