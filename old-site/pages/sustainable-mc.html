Miriam College Commitment Statement on the Sustainable Development Goals

The UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are embedded in Miriam College’s identity as an educational institution, guided by our core values of Veritas, Justice, Peace, and Integrity of Creation. 

The institution upholds our commitment to sustainability through a whole school approach, evident in its basic and higher education curricula, and in its initiatives involving the entire MC Community. 

Building on a century of academic excellence, Miriam College continues to evolve its responses to emerging challenges on sustainability and, in the face of social, economic, and environmental crises, commits to be at the forefront of promoting inclusivity, equity, and climate action.
