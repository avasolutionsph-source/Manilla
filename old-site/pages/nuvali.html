Welcome to

Miriam College Nuvali

At Miriam College Nuvali (MCN), we believe that every child has the potential to learn, grow, and make a meaningful difference in the world.

For a century, Miriam College has nurtured generations of learners in the Philippines. MC Nuvali carries that legacy forward as a co-educational campus, welcoming boys and girls from Preschool through Senior High School, and inviting them into one Miriam College community built on the same values and vision.

We provide a nurturing learning environment where students are known, supported, and encouraged to become confident in who they are and what they can achieve. Here, academic excellence goes hand in hand with character formation, creativity, leadership, and genuine care for others.

STRENGTHENING STEM

Our academic programs are focused towards honing students’ skills in STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Math) and building their interest in these fields of the future.

HOLISTIC EXPERIENCE

We promote a good balance of activities in the academic and extra-curricular areas as well as in social involvement to expand the students' learning experience and exposure.
