Welcome to

Human Resources Division

Miriam College Human Resources Division (HRD) is committed to creating a vibrant and inclusive workplace that empowers employees to reach their full potential while aligning HR strategies with the college’s overarching goals. Our key functions encompass attracting top talent through innovative recruitment, fostering continuous learning and professional growth, mediating employee relations, and managing comprehensive compensation and benefits. HRD develops and enforces policies that ensure compliance and maintain a safe work environment, oversees performance management processes, and focuses on succession planning to cultivate future leaders. Additionally, we implement culture-building programs that promote collaboration, inclusivity, and employee engagement, all aimed at enhancing retention. With a steadfast commitment to excellence, HRD endeavors to create an environment that embodies Miriam College’s core values and supports its mission of academic success and holistic development.

Our Mission

Our mission is to drive Miriam College’s success by attracting, developing, and retaining the right talent. Through strategic HR initiatives, we foster a diverse and inclusive environment that enhances employee well-being, boosts performance, and aligns with the college’s goals and values.

OUr Vision

MC is a workplace that prioritizes work-life harmony at its core, fostering a shared journey toward a distinctive employee experience characterized by a vibrant culture of collaboration, innovation, growth, and joy for everyone.

“Kasama sa Pagbuo ng Natatanging Karanasang MC”

Core Values

Employee Well-being

We prioritize the holistic well-being of our employees, recognizing that a healthy and fulfilled workforce is essential for individual and organizational success. We are committed to creating an environment that supports physical, mental, and emotional health, fostering a culture of care and support.

Agility

We emphasize the importance of quickly adapting to change and meeting evolving needs. By nurturing a culture of flexibility and innovation, we enable our team to seize opportunities and confront challenges with resilience and creativity.

Diversity and Inclusion

We embrace and celebrate the unique backgrounds and perspectives of every individual, creating a welcoming environment that drives innovation and collaboration.

Employee Development

We are committed to empowering our employees through continuous learning and growth opportunities, ensuring that everyone has the tools to reach their full potential and contribute to our shared success.

Our Purpose

The purpose of Human Resources (HR) is multifaceted, encompassing key functions that drive the overall success of Miriam College.

Talent Acquisition

HR plays a vital role in identifying, attracting, and hiring the right talent to fulfill the organization’s staffing needs. This involves developing effective recruitment strategies, conducting interviews, and managing the onboarding process.

Employee Development and Training

HR is responsible for designing and implementing programs that enhance employees’ skills, knowledge, and abilities. This includes training initiatives, career development opportunities, and performance management systems that support continuous growth.

Employee Relations and Conflict Resolution

HR acts as a mediator between employees and management, addressing issues such as grievances, disputes, and disciplinary actions. We strive to foster positive employee relations and cultivate a supportive work environment.

Compensation and Benefits Management

HR manages Miriam College’s compensation and total rewards programs, ensuring they are competitive, equitable, responsive and aligned with the organization’s goals and budget.

Policy Development and Compliance

HR develops and communicates policies and procedures that govern employee behavior and ensure compliance with relevant laws and regulations. This includes policies related to ethics, diversity, harassment prevention, and safety.

Performance Management

HR oversees the performance management process, which includes goal-setting, feedback provision, and performance evaluations. We also assist in identifying and addressing performance issues through coaching and targeted interventions.

Organizational Development

HR collaborates with senior leadership to align HR strategies with Miriam College’s overall goals and objectives. This may involve workforce planning, succession planning, and organizational restructuring to support growth and adaptation.

Succession Management

HR is responsible for succession management, ensuring that there is a robust pipeline of talent ready to step into key roles as needed. This includes identifying high-potential employees, providing development opportunities, and planning for future leadership needs.

Employee Engagement and Retention

HR is dedicated to creating a positive employee experience and fostering a culture of engagement and retention. This includes initiatives that recognize and reward employees, promote work-life harmony, and address turnover factors.

Culture Building Programs

HR designs and implements culture-building programs that promote the values and mission of Miriam College. These initiatives aim to strengthen team cohesion, enhance collaboration, and foster an inclusive and engaging workplace environment.

Overall, the purpose of HR is to optimize the potential of our human capital and create a workplace environment where employees can thrive, strive, contribute effectively, and achieve both individual and institutional goals.
