Marian Auditorium

This structure has been the hub for all artistic and cultural activities of the Maryknoll/Miriam College community and its partners since 1954. Through the years,the Marian Auditorium has been host to a multitude of presentations that celebrate human creative and intellectual expression. Significantly, these presentations – from first plays, to dancefests, songfests and concerts, to debates, honors assemblies and symposia, to graduations, and finally, alumni homecoming programs – all chronicle the various rites of passage of a young girl growing to adulthood. It is also not surprising that all through these years, it became the hub of various activities not just for the school population but for the surrounding community as well.

Residence Hall

The Miriam College Residence Hall is the school’s on-campus dormitory that can house as many as 120 students and guests. This four-story facility has two residential clusters—9 rooms located on the the first two floors are for conference or workshop guests and 22 rooms on the 2nd, 3rd and 4th floors are for senior high school and college students.

Mini-Forest Park

The Mini-Forest is a two-hectare park located at the heart of the MC Quezon City campus. It is a sanctuary for various species of birds and trees such as molave, eucalyptus, acasia, talisay, and bamboo. The Mini-Forest Park provides a peaceful and safe learning environment, and a venue for various lessons on biodiversity, waste segregation, water-sampling, quiet reflection, and peace dialogues for internal, co-sponsored and external organizations.

The design of the Mini-Forest Park was initiated by Sister Marissa Lichauco, an alumna and a Maryknoll Sister.

Room Types

Standard Room: maximum of 5 occupants with shared amenities per floor

Deluxe Room: maximum of 3 occupants with television and private toilet/bath

Features:

  • environment-friendly features such as maximum use of natural light through large glass walls and windows
  • air-conditioning in all rooms
  • living spaces are spacious and well-ventilated
  • weekly housekepping and daily garbage collection Special arrangements may also be made for laundry, postal, and other services

Shared Amenities

  • Student lounge with TV
  • Dining area with lockers
  • Toilet and bath with hot & cold showers
  • Pantry with microwave, oven toaster, refrigerator, and hot & cold drinking water dispenser
  • 24-hour Wi-Fi access
  • Intercom system in all rooms
  • Telephone ar rhe Front Desk for any local calls
  • Free weekly gym use or group class at the MC ILAW Center

Location

The RH is near other college buildings, making the classrooms, laboratories, library, internet resource center, cafeteria, and gym accessible.

Safety & Security

  • Daily operations at the RH is managed by the Front Desk staff, Dorm Mother and security guards
  • The building is has CCTV, sprinklers, smoke detectors, fire extinguishers, fire exits, and alarms
  • The clinic is available on-campus during school operations and and an ambulance service is available 24 hours.
