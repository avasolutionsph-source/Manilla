Welcome to

Miriam College Preschool

The Preschool levels at the Child Study Center (CSC) offers early childhood education for children 3 to 5 years old. Our First Step, Nursery, and Kindergarten programs provide students with a variety of developmentally appropriate activities that make them explore, investigate, discover, collaborate, create, and innovate. Through intentional teaching, they engage in meaningful and relevant activities that nurture them to become happy, confident, and lifelong learners.

Age Requirements

First Step

3 years old by September 30 of the school year being applied for

Nursery

4 years old by October 31 of the school year being applied for

Kindergarten

5 years old by December 31 of the school year being applied for

Note: Children born between November 1 and December 31 must have completed one year of Pre-Kindergarten in a school with a valid permit or recognition.

Why Choose MC Preschool?

LEARNER-CENTERED CURRICULUM

Our Thematic-Integrated and Play-Based Approach
engages children in meaningful, STEAM-inspired
activities that nurture curiosity, creativity, and
problem-solving.

AUTHENTIC ASSESSMENT OF CHILDREN’S PROGRESS

We observe and assess progress through portfolios,
performance tasks, and narrative reports that reflect
each child’s growth and learning journey.

STRONG PARTNERSHIP BETWEEN THE HOME AND THE SCHOOL

Parents are active partners in learning through
conferences, school events, and classroom
collaborations.

STIMULATING TEACHING-LEARNING ENVIRONMENT

Our classrooms and outdoor spaces are designed to
encourage exploration, communication, and
collaboration.

CELEBRATION OF MILESTONES

Children showcase what they have learned
through a choral recitation, a food and goods
bazaar, and an original Filipino children’s stage play.

EDUCATIONAL PARTNERSHIPS

We collaborate with schools and learning
communities to ensure quality, innovation, and global relevance.

Learning Areas

In the Preschool levels at the Child Study Center (CSC), we see children as true masters of wonder and imagination. These characteristics are what makes them natural creators, inventors, innovators, and problem-solvers. We develop these characteristics through the “design thinking” approach to problem-solving and equipping them with a mindset that encourages them to ask questions, empathize, work together, take risks, try out their ideas, and persevere.

What Our Parents Have to Say

KLANG ALCANTARA

When I was a little girl, I remember enjoying my pre-school days at the Child Study Center (MC-CSC) because of the cozy pajama-like uniform, the tree house, and Learning Resource Center (LRC). Never did I imagine that Miriam College would be a great part of my life.  I was a student of the Miriam College from pre-school to college, spending two years in Preschool, seven in Grade School, four in High School, and another four in college.  Miriam College truly became my second home.

REYNA MABULAY

My education in Miriam College has taught me to appreciate a well-rounded and balanced approach to learning. I’ve learned to value not only the lessons taught within the classroom setting that allow us to grow academically, but also those that we may learn beyond our daily requirements that would create an impact on our individual growth. It has empowered me to develop a more service-oriented and open-minded leadership style through the endless opportunities we get to reach out, interact, and serve with all kinds of people. MCHS holistically develops my being, so that I can be of service to others.

KAT PABIZ-CIFRA

My very first school was Miriam College Preschool. I was a Kindergarten student then in1989. I came back to CSC in 2004, the year I graduated from college, and started my teaching profession.

CSC Annual Play

The Preschool’s (Child Study Center) annual play is a unique and special year-end activity that showcases the language, cognitive, social-emotional, and physical skills that the children have gained throughout each school year. For more than 30 years, the Miriam Child Study Center has been producing and presenting all-original Filipino musical plays with the script, songs, choreography, costumes, and scenography all masterfully created and designed by Miriam Preschool dynamic team of teachers.
