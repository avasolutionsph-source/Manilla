Welcome to

miriam college-CoTEACH

The Collaborative Teacher-Assisted Classes at Home is an innovative online modality of learning offered by Miriam College for children in First Step until Grade 12. It is a response of Miriam College to the changing landscape in the Philippine educational environment that has opened itself up to the desire of parents or guardians to take a more personal and active role in the education of their children. This learning modality is available especially for students who reside in the provinces or abroad or those nursing an injury, undergoing therapy, etc. and cannot physically attend face-to-face classes regularly. Students who are part of the online sections meet their teachers via synchronous Google Meet sessions, while learning partners (parents/guardians) guide students through their lessons and tasks on the other days of the week when lessons are asynchronous.  COTEACH is open for both male and female students.
 
The Miriam College Grade School (MCGS)  curriculum will be implemented for COTEACH sections of First Step to Grade 6 while the Miriam College High School (MCHS) curriculum will be implemented in COTEACH sessions of Grades 7-12.  Both comply with the requirements of the national curriculum, including the programs and policies of  the Department of Education. 
 
Classes typically start on the last week of July & end on the last week of April. We observe Philippine holidays and school holidays. We have a 2-week Christmas break, a 1-week break for All Saints/All Souls, and a 1-week break for Holy Week.

What can we do for you?

EDUCATE

Innovative curriculum following DepEd’s Curriculum from Toddlers to Gr. 3 written by early childhood and professional teachers.

RELEVANT

Relevant, developmentally appropriate, holistic and fun learning activities that allow children to discover their strengths and pursue their interests.

FLEXIBLE

Flexible and relaxed learning time.

What we offer

EXCHANGE

LEARNING SESSIONS

3 days of synchronous sessions with a Teacher Coach and pre-service teachers of the Miriam College, College of Education.

COACHING SESSIONS

Weekly coaching sessions to guide parents in implementing the curriculum at home.

PORTFOLIO REVIEW

Monthly review of portfolios by a Teacher Coach.

DOCUMENT PROCESSING

DepEd accreditation, issuance of a transcript of records for homeschooled children, and processing of the Learner Reference Number (LRN).

What we provide

Empower

A strong student support program that addresses the emotional wellness of children. This will be provided by the MA in Guidance and Counselling program/EdD Counsellor Education Program.

The Parent Academy provides the necessary tools to build capacity and confidence in parents in order to support children academically, socially and emotionally. This is supported by the PhD Family Studies Program.
