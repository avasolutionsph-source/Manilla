Welcome to

Miriam College Alviera

Miriam College Alviera a co-educational campus, invites you to step into a learning environment specifically designed to inspire and support the next generation of innovators and leaders. As the home of the creative generation, our campus serves as a vibrant community where students can thrive across all levels of their educational journey. We are proud to offer a comprehensive range of programs, providing a seamless transition for learners from Nursery through Grade 10, as well as specialized College degree programs. Furthermore, Miriam College Alviera is distinguished as one of the campuses to offer the International Stream program in partnership with Camosun College, providing students with unique global academic pathways and international exposure.
